Make yourself comfortable.

The teacher poses a question. The student makes an observation. The teacher responds to that observation and explains the idea. The student also asks beginner follow-up questions, which the teacher answers. Reveal one message at a time. Most messages use text only. The teacher sends a diagram, code example, or experiment when it helps you understand the idea. Select an attachment to enlarge it. Source links and complete scripts are available in Resources.

Each section ends with two optional, single-choice questions. Choose an answer to see the correct answer and an explanation. Use Next or the right arrow to skip any question. Space also advances when no button is focused. Skipping does not count as an incorrect answer. Choice order is shuffled on each page load. Answers remain visible as you move back and forward in this session; reloading or restarting clears them.
